## Writing notes

- **Alternative title:** The Photos We Keep for Someday
- **Subtitle:** An unread archive can still hold memories we aren’t ready to lose.

- **Angle to explore:** The value of digital memories, collecting habits, and the consequences of losing them.
- **Indirect connection:** Why personal data matters

## Brainstorm

- **Opening:** Thousands of photos, rarely opened—but imagine discovering they were gone.
- **Explore:** Keeping a record versus revisiting it; fear of forgetting, duplicates, and the promise of “someday.”
- **Counterpoint:** Rarely viewed doesn’t mean worthless; a photo can matter years later or to someone else.
- **Possible ending:** Try a small album worth revisiting, while keeping backups and an exportable archive.
