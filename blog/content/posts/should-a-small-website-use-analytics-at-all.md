## Writing notes

- **Alternative title:** Does My Website Need to Count You?
- **Subtitle:** Curiosity about readers isn’t automatically a reason to measure them.

- **Angle to explore:** Whether learning about visitors justifies collecting information about them.
- **Indirect connection:** Privacy-conscious decisions

## Brainstorm

- **Opening:** Before installing analytics, ask what decision the numbers would actually change.
- **Explore:** Useful feedback versus vanity metrics; aggregate counts versus tracking individual visitors.
- **Counterpoint:** Measurement can reveal broken pages or useful content; server logs may already collect visitor data.
- **Possible ending:** Start without analytics, or collect only what answers a specific question, with short retention.
