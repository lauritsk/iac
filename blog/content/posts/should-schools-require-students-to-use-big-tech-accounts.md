## Writing notes

- **Alternative title:** A School Login Shouldn’t Mean Giving Up Choice
- **Subtitle:** When classroom convenience makes someone else’s platform compulsory.

- **Angle to explore:** Convenience versus students’ freedom to choose where their data goes.
- **Indirect connection:** Choice and control

## Brainstorm

- **Opening:** A student needs an account just to receive homework. Is that really a choice?
- **Explore:** Who chooses the provider, what data is required, and whether families can opt out without missing lessons.
- **Counterpoint:** Shared tools simplify teaching; alternatives also need funding, support, and security.
- **Possible ending:** Schools should justify what they require and offer a meaningful alternative where possible.
