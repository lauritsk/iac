## Writing notes

- **Alternative title:** Privacy Shouldn’t Require Expertise
- **Subtitle:** A settings menu is not the same as a fair choice.

- **Angle to explore:** How much responsibility belongs to users versus designers.
- **Indirect connection:** Technical skills and responsibility

## Brainstorm

- **Opening:** “You can turn it off” sounds reassuring—if you know what “it” does and where to find it.
- **Explore:** Confusing labels, scattered controls, changing defaults, and the time needed to keep up.
- **Counterpoint:** People want different things; useful controls and digital literacy still matter.
- **Possible ending:** Put responsibility on designers to make defaults protective and consequences understandable.
